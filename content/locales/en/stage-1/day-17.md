## Today's goal

Recall the first two notes, then add the remaining three. Avoid guessing through the full phrase repeatedly.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Review the five-note range under time pressure. Allow no more than five seconds for each note.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Wait five seconds after the target plays before trying it. Let inner hearing carry more of the memory.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Memorize five-note phrases as a 2+3 grouping, then connect the parts. Recall the first group and then the second.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Work out the fingering mentally before checking it on the guitar. Name the root and chord quality first.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record whether the first two notes or the last three were easier to lose.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice prioritizes five-note phrases that remain difficult after chunking.
