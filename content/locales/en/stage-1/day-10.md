## Today's goal

A is at fret 10 on the second string and is scale degree 6 in C major. Begin using the full five-note range.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Add A at fret 10. Review the five notes and scale degrees: C1, D2, E3, G5, A6.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Use all five notes for the first time. Targets have no accompaniment. Play C as a separate reference when needed, then identify the target's scale degree.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete five three-note phrases drawn from all five notes, not only C, E, and G.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Explain A and Am: name the root, third, and fifth, and describe how the third moves between the Em and E shapes.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record which note in the five-note range is hardest to recognize: D, G, or A.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus questions can now draw from all five notes.
