## Today's goal

Get at least eight of ten five-note questions right within two attempts and complete at least two of three unfamiliar four- or five-note phrases.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Complete ten mapping questions across the second and sixth strings.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Complete ten five-note questions and record how many were right within two attempts.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete three unfamiliar four- or five-note phrases. Record whether the first submission was correct for each.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Complete five chord questions and name the root, quality, and changed third.

## E. Weekly reflection · 5 min

Classify errors as listening, direction, position, or chord-concept errors.

## F. Focus practice · 10 min

Practice the weakest area based on your error categories.
