## Today's goal

Keep the correct pitch direction after an eight-second delay. Recall a six-note phrase by chunks, then connect it.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Locate the five notes under time pressure. Do two rounds and record the slowest note.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Wait eight seconds after the target plays before trying it. Hum silently while you wait.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Chunk, connect, and check six-note phrases. Recall each group on its own, then play the full phrase.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Switch between major and minor for five random roots and name the note that changes.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record whether you still remembered the first note after the delay.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice increases the delay and adds six-note phrases.
