## Today's goal

Run through “listen, hum, find, connect, check” once, then explain chord building aloud without looking at a diagram.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Quickly review all positions: five notes on the second string and natural roots on the sixth.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Use unfamiliar questions from the reserved pool and aim for a correct answer within two attempts.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete two six-note phrases and a passage from a song you choose, following the full listen-hum-find-connect-check sequence.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Rehearse explaining roots, thirds, fifths, and the change between major and minor.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Write down the skill you most want to stabilize tomorrow.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice includes only questions you missed today.
