## Today's goal

Name a note when you see its fret and find the fret when you see its name. Correct at least two three-note phrases after one hint.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Do two-way drills: second-string fret to note name and note name to fret. Then review sixth-string roots.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Complete eight C, E, and G questions. Track attempts for each one, not just whether it was correct.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Add quarter-note and half-note lengths to three-note phrases. Rhythm only needs to be played along; it is not scored. If you know a slow song in C major, transcribe four or five notes.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

For A, G, and C chords, identify the root, third, and fifth before looking at a fretboard diagram.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Write down your slowest step: identifying the note, finding its position, or forming the chord.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Complete the focus set. The app prioritizes the skill with the lowest accuracy.
