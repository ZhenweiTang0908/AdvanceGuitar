## Today's goal

Transcribe some notes first and use them to infer the key later. Do not stop because the key is uncertain.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Blindly draw from the five notes and sixth-string roots. Play first and name the answer afterward to check that your hands and mind agree.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Transfer your listening to an unfamiliar timbre. Identify the pitch contour before naming the exact note.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete five- or six-note phrases. Choose a song passage without confirming its key; record the notes you find rather than guessing the key.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Near your passage, find one familiar E- or Em-shape chord. You do not need to harmonize the whole section.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record which notes you found by direction and which ones you guessed.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice mixes unfamiliar timbres with note-name transfer.
