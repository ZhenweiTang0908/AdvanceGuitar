## Today's goal

Play five-note phrases with a basic rhythm. Transcribe five or six notes from a song of your choice and record their names.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Mix second- and sixth-string mapping, switching randomly between melody notes and roots.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Do not replay immediately after the first playback. Make a direction judgment first.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Play five-note phrases with a basic rhythm, then repeat along with a 60 or 72 BPM metronome. Transcribe five or six notes from a song you choose.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Switch between major and minor chords on the same root when prompted. Name only the note that changes.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record whether the tempo disrupted your pitch memory while playing along.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Repeat the weakest phrase with a slow metronome during focus practice.
