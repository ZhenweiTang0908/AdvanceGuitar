## Today's goal

Get at least 8 of 10 single-note questions right within two attempts, complete three three-note phrases, and explain the relationship between chord roots and thirds.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Complete ten mapping questions across C, E, and G on the second string and natural roots on the sixth string.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Complete ten C, E, and G questions. You may replay the target or play the C reference, but record only the attempts used for your first formal answer.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete three unfamiliar three-note phrases. Do not immediately replay phrases two and three; arrange them from memory first.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Complete five E- or Em-shape questions. Explain the root and how the major or minor third changes.

## E. Weekly reflection · 5 min

Name the skill that needs the most work: ear, phrase, fretboard, or chord.

## F. Focus practice · 10 min

Practice the area with the most errors this week. Decide whether you're ready for week 2 afterward.
