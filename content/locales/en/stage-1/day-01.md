## Today's goal

Find C, E, and G on the second string: see a note name and know its fret, or see a fret and name the note. After listening, tell whether the target is higher or lower than the note you played.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Memorize C at fret 1, E at fret 5, and G at fret 8 on the second string. On the sixth string, locate G at fret 3, A at fret 5, and C at fret 8. Play them in order, then shuffle them; say each note name before playing.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

The target plays without accompaniment. Use the separate C reference when needed. Follow this sequence: **listen → hum → find it on the guitar → submit**. After a miss, identify whether to move higher or lower before trying again.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete six two-note phrases. Listen to the full phrase, describe the relationship as “higher, lower, or same,” then arrange the answer.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Move the E shape so its root is A at fret 5 on the sixth string to make A major. Keep the position and switch to Em to make Am. For each chord, name its root, explain its name, and say which note moves down a semitone to make the minor third.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record the phrase or song you worked on, how long you practiced, and whether listening, humming, locating, or playing was hardest.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Complete the focus questions generated from your results. If no skill has results yet, the app starts with fretboard mapping.
