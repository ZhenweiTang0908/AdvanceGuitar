## Today's goal

Complete five three-note phrases. Then choose a song and find a four-note fragment, writing down the note names even if you don't know the key yet.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Call out the sixth-string roots F, G, A, B, and C in random order. Play each one, name it, then find the corresponding scale degree on the second string.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Wait three seconds after the target plays before trying it. The pause gives your inner hearing time to work before your fingers take over.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete five three-note phrases. Then play a familiar slow song and transcribe the first four notes of its main melody. Hum first, find the notes, then write their names. The song does not need to be in C major.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Compare open A and Am with movable A and Am shapes rooted on the sixth string. The root stays the same; the third determines major or minor.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record the song, timestamp, and notes you found, for example “0:12–0:16: G, A, B, A.”

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Complete your focus set. If phrase accuracy is low, the app prioritizes two-note contours.
