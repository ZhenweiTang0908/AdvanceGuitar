## Today's goal

Reproduce the same phrase after changing its timbre; don't rely only on piano or plucked sounds.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Review the five notes in random order. Read the note name first, then find its position.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Mix piano, plucked, and pure tones in one question set. Change timbre every two questions.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete four- and five-note phrases across timbres. State the first note's direction, then arrange the complete answer.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Compare major and minor quality only; do not add new chord types.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record which timbre made the second half of a phrase easiest to forget.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice continues to mix timbres and prioritizes phrase work.
