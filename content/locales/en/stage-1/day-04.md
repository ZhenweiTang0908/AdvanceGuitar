## Today's goal

Locate D at fret 10 and E at fret 12 on the sixth string. Hum the correct direction before playing for at least half of the single-note questions.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Complete the natural-note root map on the sixth string. Call a random root and its fret before playing to check yourself.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Hum before touching the guitar. Even if the pitch is not exact, keep the direction. Find the note only after humming.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Recall three-note phrases that include a repeated note. Repetition makes contour memory important; don't treat the phrase as three unrelated pitches.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

After hearing a root and major/minor quality, choose its E or Em movable shape on the sixth string. Say the root before forming the shape.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Name the exact step that failed: “I can hum it but can't find it,” or “I can find it but can't name it.”

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Complete adaptive practice, prioritizing the skill that took the most attempts today.
