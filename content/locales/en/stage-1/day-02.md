## Today's goal

For each of eight direction questions, recover from a miss by stating “higher” or “lower” on the next turn. Find F at fret 1 and B at fret 7 on the sixth string.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Review both directions between C, E, and G on the second string, then add F and B on the sixth. Shuffle the order; don't rely on reciting notes from low to high.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Targets have no accompaniment. Play the separate C reference when you choose. After each target, state its direction relative to the previous note before touching the guitar. Keep a direction record even when you miss; that is closer to real transcription than replaying alone.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Practice three two-note relationships: same, step, and skip. Same repeats a note; a step moves to an adjacent note; a skip crosses over one.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Build G/Gm and C/Cm. Compare the third: a major third is a major third above the root, while the minor third is one semitone lower.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Write down one specific sticking point, such as “I know E, but I react slowly to its position on the second string.”

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus on the skill with the most errors. If direction questions are the weakest, the app returns to single-note work.
