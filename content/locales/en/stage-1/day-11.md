## Today's goal

Locate each of the five melody notes within five seconds. Transcribe five notes from a song you choose and write their names.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Locate the five notes on the second string under time pressure. Think of the fret before playing after seeing a note name.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Answer ten higher/lower questions across the five-note range. Decide whether the target is above or below the reference before identifying the note.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete four four-note phrases. Transcribe five notes from a song of your choice and record the note names.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Randomize the root and major/minor quality. Say the chord name before forming its shape.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record which of the five notes took the longest to locate.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice prioritizes the note that was slowest to locate.
