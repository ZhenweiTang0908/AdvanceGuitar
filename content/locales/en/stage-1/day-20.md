## Today's goal

Memorize a six-note phrase as two groups of three. Transcribe five or six notes from a song you choose.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Review the full range: five notes on the second string and natural roots on the sixth.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Complete ten five-note questions with mixed timbres. Judge direction before finding the exact note.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Practice a six-note phrase by grouping the notes. Also transcribe a passage from a song and record positions and note names.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Locate chord tones at random and name the root, third, and fifth.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record where in the six-note phrase you are most likely to lose the sequence.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice prioritizes six-note phrases and single-note direction judgments.
