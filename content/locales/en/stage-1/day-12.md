## Today's goal

Get at least eight of ten five-note questions correct within two attempts and describe the direction of a phrase.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Translate a sequence of frets into note names and scale degrees. For example, frets 1–3–5–8 on the second string are C–D–E–G.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Complete ten five-note questions. After your first attempt, state whether the target was higher or lower before trying a second time.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete five four-note phrases with rising, falling, or changing contours.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

See a sixth-string root position and name its chord immediately. Start with the root rather than the shape.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record your percentage correct within two attempts and whether the second attempt used a clear direction.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice prioritizes questions that took three or more attempts.
