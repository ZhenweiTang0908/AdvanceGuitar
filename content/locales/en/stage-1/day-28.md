## Today's goal

Complete every assessment section independently. If you miss a target, the app recommends a three-day focus package instead of locking later content.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Answer ten random cues. Locate at least eight within five seconds.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Answer ten questions across the five-note range. Get at least eight right within two attempts.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete three unfamiliar four- to six-note phrases. Get at least two fully correct before revealing the answer; on the third, use direction feedback to correct no more than one note.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

For five random roots and major/minor qualities, get at least four shapes, names, and roots right. Explain how the third moves down a semitone from E to Em.

## E. Written reflection · 5 min

Record your biggest improvement in Stage 1 and the question you most want to tackle next.

## F. Results and focus practice · 10 min

Review your result. If you did not pass, complete the three-day focus package. If you passed, continue to the Stage 2 roadmap; Stage 2 lessons are outside this release.
