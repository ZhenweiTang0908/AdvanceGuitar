## Today's goal

Get at least eight of ten five-note questions right within two attempts, complete at least one of two four-note phrases, and meet this week's fretboard and chord goals.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Map five second-string notes and natural sixth-string notes. Aim for at least eight correct out of ten.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Complete ten questions across the five-note range. Record how many were correct within two attempts.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete two unfamiliar four-note phrases. The first submission for each counts as the formal result.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Complete five random questions using E and Em shapes.

## E. Weekly reflection · 5 min

Write down the one skill most worth practicing next.

## F. Focus practice · 10 min

Practice the lowest-scoring area, then move on to week 3.
