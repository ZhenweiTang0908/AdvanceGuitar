## Today's goal

Complete five four-note phrases that include a repeated note, and identify which note repeats.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Map the five notes on the second string in both directions: note name to fret and fret to note name.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Distinguish repeated notes from neighboring notes. First decide whether note two matches note one, then whether it is adjacent.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete five four-note phrases with a repeated note. Describe the contour before arranging the answer.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

For three randomly selected barre chords, identify the root, third, and fifth in order.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record whether repeated notes helped you remember the contour or made the second half less clear.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice now mixes piano, plucked, and pure tones.
