## Today's goal

D is at fret 3 on the second string and is scale degree 2 in C major. Answer eight single-note questions using C, D, E, and G.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Add D at fret 3 on the second string. Review C, E, and G and name each note's scale degree in C major.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Answer eight questions using C, D, E, and G. D and E are close together, so pay special attention to their direction.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Practice short phrases using two or three notes from the four-note set. Describe the contour first as steps or skips.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Build D and Dm from a D root. D is at fret 10 on the sixth string; the Em shape gives Dm, and the E shape gives D major.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Note whether D and E are easy to confuse and whether the confusion happens while listening or locating the note.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Complete the focus set. D is now part of the question pool.
