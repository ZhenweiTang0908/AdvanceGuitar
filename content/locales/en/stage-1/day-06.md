## Today's goal

Give an answer after the first playback, then decide whether to listen again. Freely switch between major and minor shapes rooted at F, B, and D.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Locate natural notes on the sixth string under time pressure. Allow five seconds per note; mark anything slower for review.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Answer before replaying the target. This reveals real uncertainty instead of making the same question familiar through repetition.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete three-note phrases and transcribe a four- or five-note fragment from a song you choose. Real songs are not limited to C, E, and G.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Switch randomly between E and Em shapes rooted at F, B, and D. Say the chord name before each switch.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record how often you replayed and which questions made you want to reveal the answer immediately.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Complete your focus set. If single-note accuracy is low, the app adds more direction-feedback questions.
