## Today's goal

Recognize the direction of the same target after its timbre changes. Explain that changing a major third to a minor third lowers the third by one semitone.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Mix second-string notes and sixth-string roots. Name each note before playing it.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Hear the same target in piano and plucked tones. Compare the timbres first, then identify pitch.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete four-note phrases and transcribe five or six notes from a song you choose. Note which pitches you found by direction.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Without a diagram, explain which string or note moves when a major third becomes a minor third.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record whether the timbre change caused more errors and which timbre was hardest.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice mixes timbres and prioritizes single-note questions.
