## Today's goal

When D and E appear in sequence, find each using direction and known-note references instead of memorizing answers.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Shuffle C, D, E, and G. Find the fret from a note name, then name the note from its fret.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Focus on D and E. Hear a known C first, then the target, and decide whether it is a second or a third above C.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete four four-note phrases. Then choose a song with a clear vocal or single-note melody and transcribe four or five notes.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Name major or minor chords at random natural roots on the sixth string. Say each chord name before forming its shape.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record whether D/E confusion has decreased and which reference note helped you find D.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice prioritizes comparisons between D and E.
