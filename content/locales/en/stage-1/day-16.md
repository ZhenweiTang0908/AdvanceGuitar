## Today's goal

Return to the same fretboard area from a note name, scale degree, or fret cue. Transcribe five notes from a song and compare the contour.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Shuffle note-name, scale-degree, and fret cues. State your answer before playing.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Practice two consecutive direction judgments. If the first pitch is wrong, use its feedback to move on the second attempt.

## C. Phrase recall and mini transcription · 15 min

Complete four- or five-note phrases. Transcribe five notes from a song you choose and compare its contour.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Alternate between open and higher-position versions of the same chord. Compare the root position and shape.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record which cue was slowest: note name, scale degree, or fret.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice rotates among all three cue types.
