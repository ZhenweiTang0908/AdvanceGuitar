## Today's goal

Answer ten random-timbre questions in no more than three attempts, and build and name a chord within ten seconds.

## A. Fretboard mapping · 8 min

Translate note names and fret positions quickly, taking no more than five seconds per cue.

## B. Single-note ear training · 12 min

Answer ten questions with random timbres. After two attempts, decide whether to continue, and record every attempt honestly.

## C. Phrase recall · 15 min

Complete five five-note phrases. Describe each note's direction before your first submission.

## D. Build chords · 10 min

Given a random root and major/minor quality, form the shape and explain its name within ten seconds.

## E. Reflection · 5 min

Record how many questions were right within two attempts and your average attempts.

## F. Adaptive practice · 10 min

Focus practice prioritizes skills that took more than two attempts.
