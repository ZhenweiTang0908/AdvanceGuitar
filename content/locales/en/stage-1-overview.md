# Stage 1 overview

Over 28 days, connect three questions: **What is the sound, where is it on the fretboard, and what role does it play in C major?** Plan for about one hour each day, including fretboard work, ear training, phrase work or transcription, chord building, reflection, and adaptive practice.

## Four-week roadmap

| Week | Focus | By the end of the week |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Connect C, E, and G to nearby fretboard positions | Find the three notes within two attempts and complete three-note phrases |
| 2 | Add D and A to create a five-note range | Identify at least 80% of five-note questions within two attempts |
| 3 | Move from single notes to four- to six-note phrases | Reproduce four- or five-note phrases and follow a basic rhythm |
| 4 | Reduce attempts and complete the assessment | Complete the fretboard, single-note, phrase, and chord checks independently |

## How to practice each day

Days are recommended in order, but none are locked. Preview any lesson or work on several skills in one day. Progress depends on how you perform on new questions, not on repeating the same question until it feels familiar.

When you use a real song, choose a recording with a clear melody and a relaxed tempo. The app does not provide or store copyrighted songs; use it to note song locations, notes, and reflections.
